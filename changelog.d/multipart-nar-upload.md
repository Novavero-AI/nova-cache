- **A NAR can be uploaded in parts, past a proxy's limit on request bodies: `POST /nar/<file>/uploads` begins an upload, `PUT /nar/<file>/uploads/<id>/<n>` sends part `n`, and `POST /nar/<file>/uploads/<id>` joins the parts into `nar/<file>`.** A NAR went up in one `PUT /nar/<file>`, and a proxy in front of the server can refuse a body over its own limit, as Cloudflare does over 100 MB on its Free and Pro plans, so a larger NAR could not reach a cache behind one. Upstream's `HttpBinaryCacheStore` also sends each file in one `PUT` (2.35.6), and only its S3 store uploads in parts, through S3's own API, so there is no upstream HTTP protocol to match. The requests follow S3's multipart upload in plain paths: the server issues the upload's id, parts are numbered from 1, a part sent again replaces the one held, and the completion declares the total size, as S3's optional `x-amz-mp-object-size` does. Each request is authenticated as the other writes are. Parts are staged under `uploads/`, outside `nar/`, each streamed to disk in constant memory and renamed into place whole, and they go in order: a part that would leave a gap is refused with 409. The completion's body declares the part count and the total size, as `Parts:` and `FileSize:` lines in narinfo's form, and is refused with 409 unless the server holds exactly that; only then are the parts joined and the NAR renamed into `nar/`, so an upload in progress or abandoned never appears there, and the narinfo check verifies the joined file's `FileSize` and `FileHash` as before. An upload's parts together are held to the 4 GB of a single `PUT`, checked as each part arrives and again at completion, since two parts written at once can each pass the first check; an upload holds at most 10,000 parts, S3's limit, and a name too long to stage (its directory's name is the NAR's with the 32-digit id before it) is refused with 400. A reclaim also removes an upload nothing has been written to for the minimum age: no narinfo can name a staged upload's parts, so removing them never leaves a narinfo without its NAR. `newFileStore` creates `uploads/`. The library gains the upload's store operations and types in `NovaCache.Store` (`beginUpload`, `writeUploadPart`, `completeUpload`, `listStagedUploads`, `removeUpload`), `selectAbandoned` in `NovaCache.Reclaim`, and the completion body's `parseUploadTotals` and `renderUploadTotals` in `NovaCache.Server`.
